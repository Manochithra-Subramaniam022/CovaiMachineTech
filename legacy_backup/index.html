About Covai Machine Tech

ISO 9001:2015 Certified Precision CNC Manufacturing Facility

ISO 9001:2015 CERTIFIED COMPANY

Certificate No: 25MEQTH25 | Valid Thru: 26/02/2028

Scope: Manufacturing & Supply of Machined Components for Automobile and General Engineering.

Covai Machine Tech is an ISO 9001:2015 certified precision CNC manufacturing facility situated on Annur Main Road, Near Suzlon, Kittampalayam, Karumathampatti, Coimbatore. Spanning an expansive 2-Acre facility with 7,000 Sq.Ft of constructed workspace, we are equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure and well-trained operators.

We are a team of qualified professionals with a proficient technical background, undertaking all kinds of challenging projects. We thoroughly understand customer requirements and adhere to precise planning and execution with a firm emphasis on quality and timely delivery at optimal cost.

Our core capabilities include machining various valve components such as ball valves, gate valves, globe valves, check valves, and other pipeline-related parts. We undertake complete and partial job work based on client drawings, specifications, and industry standards, supported by our own dedicated transportation facilities for collection and delivery.

WHAT WE DO

Our Core Capabilities

Comprehensive CNC machining, multi-axis turning, and precision manufacturing solutions

LMW Turning

CNC Turning Operations

Advanced high-speed turning using LMW LL20TL5 & LL20TL10 centers (with steady rest) for precision cylindrical components up to 1100mm turning length.

3 & 4 Axis VMC

Vertical Machining (VMC)

High-precision 3-Axis and 4-Axis (U-CAM) machining using BFW, AMS (1800mm travel), and KTM Double Column Machining Centers with through-coolant & angular head.

Heavy Duty VTL

Vertical Turning Lathe

Heavy-duty precision turning up to 800mm swing diameter and 700mm turning length using BFW BVL 700H for large industrial and valve components.

Valve Machining

Valve & Pipeline Parts

Specialist machining of ball valves, gate valves, globe valves, check valves, and pipeline engineering parts in carbon steel, stainless steel, alloy steel, and Inconel.

CARL ZEISS CMM

CMM Quality Inspection

Rigorous quality control utilizing CARL ZEISS CONTURA 3D CMM, Trimos V7 2D Height Gauge (up to 1023mm), Mahr Surface Roughness tester, and Mitutoyo digital gauges.

CAD & Logistics

Job Work & Logistics

Undertaking complete and partial job work with customized value-added services, supported by our dedicated transportation facilities for collection and delivery of materials.
